PhishLens

Hackathon Winner

An explainable phishing-detection dashboard built for college students during a TCS hackathon. It blends a Random Forest classifier, a weighted keyword engine and link-reputation analysis into a single traceable risk score, then surfaces concrete evidence such as impersonated senders, shortened links and urgency or payment language. A language-model layer narrates the verdict in plain English without influencing it, so every point of the score traces back to something visible in the email.
